# Measured Clip Durations and Estimated Word Triggers {#sec:synchronization}

## Measured Durations, Estimated Word Boundaries

DemoCreate measures the duration of each generated narration WAV. The default `HeuristicTranscriber` distributes the known narration text over that duration in proportion to character length, with a one-character minimum weight. These word boundaries are interpolated estimates, **not measured acoustic onsets**. Installing a real-voice TTS backend does not change that distinction.

The silent backend writes a real WAV whose duration is derived from the requested speaking rate. System and Kokoro TTS provide speech when their prerequisites are available. `synthesize_demo` writes numbered `chunk_NNNN.wav` files; chunk IDs remain opaque identifiers rather than path components. Synchronization records measured `Chunk.duration_ms`; `playback_duration_ms(wpm=150)` uses that measurement when available and otherwise estimates duration.

## Trigger Matching, Fallbacks, and Authored Absolute Times

`sync_demo` matches an action's `trigger_word` to a word in its chunk and assigns an absolute timestamp from the local estimate and cumulative chunk offset. Missing triggers fall back to the chunk start. Fuzzy matching tolerates punctuation and modest spelling differences; it does not recognize speech. Complete or monotonic timestamps establish structural properties only: they do not prove a trigger matched or that a visible event coincides with a spoken word.

Explicit untriggered authored timestamps remain absolute. Sync-derived times
carry `timestamp_source="sync"` so re-synthesis refreshes them after narration
edits; serialized resolved Demos retain that distinction. WAIT advances the
fallback cursor, and captured discrete inputs use zero-duration events.

## One Timebase for Clip Windows, Gaps, and Visual Replay

Audio concatenation and animation reserve the same lead, inter-chunk and trail silences. This aligns clip windows, chapter boundaries and waveform progress to one timebase. It does not establish within-chunk acoustic alignment. Action timestamps and actual rendered event timing must be evaluated separately against annotated speech.

## Unwired Recognition and Independent Onset Evaluation

`WhisperTranscriber` is an **unimplemented adapter slot**, not a working upgrade: dependency availability does not implement recognition. Whisper [@radford2023whisper] and alignment systems such as WhisperX [@whisperx2023] are relevant future implementations, not measured DemoCreate capabilities.

The independent voiced-event study and its blocking corpus/error criteria are defined in [@sec:evaluation] and `experiment_plan.yaml`. Annotation must assess actual encoded visible events against independent speech onsets, not compare timestamps generated by the same heuristic. The silent default is an engineering control: it has no acoustic word onsets, so its acoustic error is undefined, not zero.
